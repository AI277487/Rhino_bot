Privacy Policy · Last updated: August 2026

Artai operates RhinoBot, an educational reference tool that answers otolaryngology (ENT) questions grounded in standard medical textbooks. This policy explains what information we collect when you use RhinoBot, why we collect it, and how it is handled. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legal language.

Who we are

RhinoBot is provided by Artai. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at support.artai@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your name, as provided by your Google account.
  • Your email address, as provided by your Google account.

We also store the questions you ask RhinoBot and the answers it returns, together with the time they were asked. We do not collect your Google password, your contacts, your files, or any other data from your Google account beyond your name and email.

Why we collect it

  • To sign you in. Your name and email identify your account so you can use the tool and return to it.
  • To operate the service fairly. We record questions to apply usage limits, prevent abuse, and understand which topics are asked about so the tool can be improved.
  • To improve RhinoBot. Reviewing questions (and where answers fell short) helps us improve the underlying reference material.

How your data is stored

Account information and query records are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, a third-party infrastructure provider, on secured servers. Access is restricted to the operator of the service. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your data is processed only by the service providers that make RhinoBot work — namely Google (for sign-in), Supabase (for data storage), and Anthropic (whose models generate answers; the text of your question is sent to Anthropic to produce a response). These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists, and query records for as long as they remain useful for operating and improving the service. You may ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting the email above.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the service and sign out at any time.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us.
  • You can revoke RhinoBot's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

A note on medical content

RhinoBot is an educational and exam-preparation reference, not a medical device and not a substitute for professional clinical judgment. Its answers should not be used for diagnosis or treatment of any individual patient. Always rely on qualified clinical advice for patient care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of RhinoBot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
